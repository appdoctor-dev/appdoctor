**Screenshots** in the sidebar holds four designs for each app: iPhone, iPad, the product page header and the search result art.

## Designs

| Design | Canvas | Exports |
| --- | --- | --- |
| iPhone | 1320 × 2868 | 6.9" and 6.5" |
| iPad | 2064 × 2752 | 13" and 12.9" |
| Page header | 3840 × 1646 (21:9) | Image or video |
| Search result | 3840 × 2560 (3:2) | Image or video |

A design is a row of slides. Each slide has a background and layers: text, devices, images and shapes.

- **Devices:** every iPhone from iPhone X to the latest, and every iPad, in each finish or with no frame. Drop in a screenshot or a screen recording of your app.
- **Text:** any font installed on your computer, with lines broken where the export will break them.
- **Across slides:** a layer that runs past one screenshot's edge carries on into the next, for panoramas.
- **Drawings:** add an SVG (an AI can draw one for you) and each part becomes its own layer to move, resize and edit.

**Export PNGs** writes every screenshot at the sizes App Store Connect asks for.

## App preview videos

Turn on **Make a video** in the inspector with nothing selected. Slides become scenes, joined by a push, fade or cut.

- Each layer can enter, leave and drift: fade, rise, slide, zoom or pop.
- A device can play a screen recording of your app.
- Add synthesized sound effects, or your own music and voice-over.
- **Export video** writes an H.264 MP4: 886 × 1920 for iPhone or 1200 × 1600 for iPad, 15 to 30 seconds, at 30 frames a second, as App Store Connect expects. Video export needs macOS for now.

## With your AI

Click **AI** in the Screenshots header and ask for what you want: "Design 5 screenshots with short benefit headlines", or "Make a 20-second app preview of the app's best features". The AI can record your app in the iOS Simulator for the device screens. See [Use AppDoctor with AI](/docs/ai-agents).
