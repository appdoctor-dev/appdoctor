> This is coming in an upcoming release. The page describes how it will work.

AppDoctor includes a local MCP server, so an AI agent running on your computer can use your AppDoctor data the way you do. Nothing is sent to us. The agent talks to the app on your machine, and the app talks to Apple.

## Connect an agent

In AppDoctor, open **Settings → AI agents** and choose your client (Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Codex, Cursor or any MCP client). AppDoctor shows the one-line setup for it.

## Things to ask

- "Which of my keywords have high popularity and low difficulty in Germany?"
- "Localize my App Store listing into German, French, Japanese and Brazilian Portuguese."
- "Rewrite my subtitle around the searches I'm ranking 11–30 for."
- "Attach build 412 to version 2.3 and get it ready for review."

## What the agent can and can't do

- It can read your apps, keywords, ranks and listing text.
- Listing changes are saved to a draft version, never straight to the live App Store.
- Submitting for review always shows you a summary (app, version, build) first, and only goes ahead after you confirm.
